# Sound credits

The café ambience in Pit Wall On-Call uses two recordings from Wikimedia Commons. Both are free to use without conditions. They are credited here anyway, with thanks. Every other sound in the game is synthesized in the browser.

| File | Source | Author | Licence | What we use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cafe-murmur.mp3` | [Cafe ambiance.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cafe_ambiance.ogg) | Marble Toast | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) | 45 s from 8:36, mono, 64 kbps, loudness-normalized |
| `rain-window.mp3` | [Rain against the window.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_against_the_window.ogg), originally from PDSounds | cori | Public domain | 30 s from 0:39, mono, 64 kbps, loudness-normalized |

`scripts/ambience.mjs` makes these trims from the originals.
