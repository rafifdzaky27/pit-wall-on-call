# M2.5 PR B: Tools and Teammates Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Chat is something you use. You ask teammates and they answer after a while, sometimes wrongly; you post status updates in your own words. The work happens in the tool a real SRE would open:
- **Logs:** searching.
- **Deploys:** history, rollback and restart.
- **DB console:** connections, failover and `max_connections`.
- **Incident:** status, timeline, status page and paging.

Monitoring keeps the dashboards, and everything links to everything else.

**Architecture:**
- **Engine:** gains asynchronous actions. They never block the player, and their effect and findings land when their time is up. `ENGINE_VERSION` becomes 1.1.0.
- **Scenario actions:** gain `tool` (where the action lives) and `ask` (a teammate question: to whom, the prompt, and a slash-command topic).
- **Chat:**
  - renders the player's own messages from the timeline;
  - offers suggested replies (chips) per conversation;
  - parses slash commands.
- **New lazy apps:** Logs, Deploys, DB console and Incident. They read the same incident API and dispatch the same engine actions.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript ~6, React 19, Vite 8, Vitest 5, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-09-28-m2.5-clear-connected-alive-design.md` §7, §8, D3 (and §14 Review Focus 4 and 5).

## Global Constraints

- **Engine:** golden scores do not change. An action log that was valid under 1.0.0 replays to the same result under 1.1.0; test this with the golden players and the M2 fixture run.
- **API:** its `ENGINE_VERSION` follows the engine, so stale clients get 409. The smoke fixture is unchanged.
- **Copy:** English, sentence case. Teammates' chat is in-world and may be casual. No emoji or decorative icons in PitOS chrome (reactions in chat are in-world, as before).
- **Budgets:** new apps are lazy chunks, and the main chunk grows by at most 3 KB gzip.
- **Viewport:** every tool is usable maximised at 1366×657.
- **Commits:** conventional messages with no AI attribution trailers.

## Decisions made in this plan

| # | Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Async semantics | `ActionDef.async: true` starts at dispatch, never sets `busy`, completes at `start + duration`, and cannot be dispatched again while pending (`pending` reject reason). | A question to a teammate should not freeze the console. |
| B2 | Existing actions | `global.ask_secondary` becomes async. Every other existing action keeps blocking. | A valid 1.0.0 log never dispatched during that action's busy window, so its results are identical. |
| B3 | Player messages | Derived from the timeline (`action_start` of an `ask` action) plus a presentation-only store for the free text of `/status` and free chat. | Replay stays the only source of truth for what happened. |
| B4 | Tools | `ActionDef.tool: "dashboards" \| "logs" \| "deploys" \| "db" \| "incident" \| "chat"`. Monitoring shows only `dashboards` actions, plus "Open in <tool>" links per service. | One home per action, like a real toolchain. |
| B5 | Free text | Free chat text that is not a command posts as the player's message. The addressed teammate answers "Not sure what you mean. Try /help or one of the suggestions." after 5 s (presentation only). | Honest about what the game understands. |

## Review Focus
1. An old (1.0.0-shape) log with `global.ask_secondary` followed by an action inside its former busy window: that log was invalid before, so it may now be valid, and that is fine. A log valid before must replay identically (Task 1 test).
2. Two asks to different teammates can be pending at once, and each reply arrives on time (Task 1).
3. A slash command with a wrong name or topic gives an inline error in the composer, not a message (Task 3).
4. Every action of every scenario lives in exactly one tool, and each tool renders it (Task 4 test).
5. The e2e flows reach the rollback through Deploys (Task 6).

### Task 3: Chat you can use

**Files:** `apps/web/src/os/apps/chat/` (a `Composer.tsx`, `commands.ts` and `playerMessages.ts`), and tests.

- [ ] **Failing tests:**
  - a DM with a teammate shows their ask chips; clicking one posts the player's message at once and dispatches the action; the typing indicator shows; the reply appears when the action completes;
