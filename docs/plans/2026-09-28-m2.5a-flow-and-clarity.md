# M2.5 PR A: Flow and Clarity Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A player always knows what state the incident is in and what to do next. There is:
- a status chip;
- a self-ticking checklist and Help;
- a rubber-duck hint;
- a guided training shift;
- a results card that opens by itself.

New shift and the cold close glide instead of jumping, and a tab from before a deploy never crashes.

**Architecture:**
- **Engine:** the snapshot gains a derived `status`.
- **Incident provider:** becomes a hub. A keyed `Session` reports its API upward, so the Stage (camera and café) and the submission state live above the per-shift remount. Only the OS and its apps (`ShiftScope`, keyed by seed) remount on New shift.
- **Scenarios:** gain a `duck` action with hints and a training scenario with coach steps.
- **New UI:** Help, the coach card and the results card are new UI in the web app.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript ~6, React 19, Vite 8, Vitest 5, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-09-28-m2.5-clear-connected-alive-design.md` §3–§6, §9–§11 (and §14 Review Focus 1, 2, 3, 5).

## Global Constraints

- **Engine:**
  - `ENGINE_VERSION` stays `1.0.0` in PR A. `status` is read-only, and golden scores do not change (par 450, perfect 253 bp on seed 1).
  - New actions are scenario content, and existing action logs replay identically.
- **Copy:** English, sentence case, exactly as the spec. No emoji or decorative icons in PitOS chrome.
- **Motion:** only `transform` and `opacity`, and everything becomes a cut under reduced motion.
- **Storage:** `localStorage` is always wrapped in try/catch.
- **Budgets:** the main chunk grows by at most 4 KB gzip (86,378 B on `main`). Help, the results card and the training content load lazily.
- **Commits:** conventional messages with no AI attribution trailers.

## Decisions made in this plan

| # | Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The duck's cost | A timed scenario action (`duck`, 30 s, busy). Its hints come from a per-scenario list, in order (`state.ducks` counts them). | No engine change; it replays like any action. |
| A2 | Status triggers for chat | A `{ kind: "status"; status; afterTicks }` message trigger, evaluated in the web app from the tick the status began. It is presentation only. | Chat is not scored, so it need not replay. |
| A3 | Where the results card lives | The Stage's café UI layer. Its "Full leaderboard" shows the board inside the card, not in the Browser. | The card sits above the per-shift scope and cannot reach the OS. |
| A4 | Submission reset | `SubmissionProvider` moves above the Stage and resets itself when the seed changes. | The results card needs it; a remount would take the Stage with it. |
| A5 | Coach "Show me" | Controls carry `data-coach="<step>"`; Show me outlines the first visible match for 3 s. | It is decoupled from each app's internals. |

## Review Focus
1. A mitigation without a fix shows "Mitigated · cause still active" at once, and the secondary's nudge follows within 20 s (Task 2 and Task 3 tests).
2. New shift from the café, from the laptop and in the middle of a camera move lands on the desktop with no app open; the Stage element is the same DOM node before and after (Task 4 test).
3. A failed lazy chunk reloads the page instead of showing the crash screen (Task 6 test).
4. The results card fits at 1366×657 with New shift visible (Task 5 e2e).
5. The training shift is never posted, and the real shift after it is (Task 8 test).
