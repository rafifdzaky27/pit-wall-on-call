# M1.6 persona walkthrough: the café cold open

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Build:** branch `feat/m1.6-cafe-cold-open`, production build served by `vite preview`
- **Method:** Claude ran a scripted Playwright walkthrough (not a usability test with people). Following the M1.5 lesson, every persona plays **to the end of the run**, through the fix hold, the cold close and the postmortem. The incident clock is fast-forwarded with Playwright's clock; the camera's own animations run in real time. There is a screenshot per step, and console errors are logged.

## Findings

| # | Grade | What happened | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Important | The laptop's live screen painted over the phone close-up and its dimmed backdrop (Dina). | The layer rule is café art, then the laptop screen (1), then the café UI (2). There is an e2e check of paint order at the centre of the laptop screen. |
| W2 | Important | "Look up" became a fourth column of the three-column top bar, and the tray (On call, phone, volume, power) wrapped onto a second row over window title bars (Dina, Arif). | Look up moved inside the tray. A unit test checks that the bar keeps three children. |

Both fixes were verified by re-running the whole walkthrough.

## What worked

- No console errors on any path.
- **Tab order in the café:** Laptop, Phone, The next table, Poster on the wall, Radio, then Skip to the page and Back to laptop (L). Focus rings are visible.
- **Captions** for the next table and the poster read naturally. The phone close-up shows the page and the mention, with a clock in the city's time zone.
- **The fix hold** shows "Fix holding · 6 s" with its bar. The cold close focuses "Read the postmortem". The postmortem shows "Mitigated 00:40 · Confirmed 00:50", and 1/3 clues after one overheard clue.
- **The late close** (after 8 minutes, a DNF) is a clear night: the rain has stopped, the next table is empty, and the coffee has gone cold.
