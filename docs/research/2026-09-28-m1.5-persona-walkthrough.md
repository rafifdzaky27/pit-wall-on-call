# M1.5 persona walkthrough (2026-09-28)

## Method

The 5-person usability test in `m1.5-usability-protocol.md` did not run, because nobody had time to recruit participants. This walkthrough closes M1.5 in its place (decision S11 in `docs/specs/2026-09-28-pitos-polish-design.md`).

- **Build:** production `https://pitwall.rafifdzaky.com` after the M1.5 merge (#17).
- **Tools:**
  - A Playwright script played each persona's likely path at that persona's screen size, capturing screenshots and layout measurements.
  - Rafif's own play session on Edge at 1866×882 added two screenshots and a list of issues.
- **Personas** (from the desktop spec §3):
  - Dina: junior DevOps, 1366×768 laptop, about 657 px of viewport
  - Arif: student, 1440×900, about 789 px
  - Maya: senior SRE, 1920×1080, about 950 px

**Limits.** A scripted walkthrough finds layout, flow and heuristic problems (Nielsen's 10). It cannot show what real people misunderstand, how they feel, or where they hesitate. No SUS score is reported, because any number would be invented. Testing with real people is moved to "After launch" research in the roadmap.

## Re-run after M1.5.1 (2026-09-28)

The same three personas ran on the M1.5.1 preview build at the same sizes, with screenshots at each step. No console errors in any run.

| # | Finding | Status | Evidence |
